Warp Speed is Palantir’s manufacturing OS. FDSE sits with the plant from shop floor to board room. The IPS artifacts are that job on paper: what the line actually runs, where the bottleneck is (often I/O and journals, not more boxes), how to pick an upgrade type, and who on the floor can run it after I fly out.

What the artifacts mean here

Documentation the shift can run

Environment Summary, Upgrade Roadmap, Phase review. Not a logo slide. Owner, date, evidence, what was changed, what is still on fire. Custom apps die. Runbooks stay.

Performance the line can feel

Baseline → bottleneck → eliminate. Mercedes-Benz Financial: 20–30% off job runtime by caching in RAM. Waddell & Reed: disk wait 30–90 ms while RAM sat idle. Do not buy more boxes until you have measured the floor.

03

Coaching so I can leave

Named skill-up. Walked ALFA→ODS loads. Whole stack plus GCS in one room. If the night shift cannot run it, I did not finish. That is FDSE culture with receipts.
